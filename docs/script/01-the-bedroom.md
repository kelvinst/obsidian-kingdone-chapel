# Act 1 — The bedroom

It is dark. I wake on the floor, face down, my cheek against cold wood. I cough, and the dust rises in a cloud. It is on everything.

I get up slowly. It is a bedroom. There is a bed beside me, the linen buried in dust, untouched for far too long. A high window lets in a thin, slanted light, with dust floating inside it.

I woke where I was supposed to wake. Only on the floor, and far too late.

I know who I am. I know my own hands, I know the sound of my own voice when I cough. What I do not know is this room, and I do not know how long I have been lying in it.

That is when I saw the book. Open on the floor, a few steps away, with something written on the page.

I crouch. I pick it up. It says, in a small, tidy hand:

> i woke on the floor and i do not know where i am

I read it again. I read it once more, trying to understand how that can be there. And then, while I hold the book in my hand, a new letter appears. Then another. One by one, no quill, no hand, nobody.

I shut the book with a start and almost drop it. I stand back watching it, breathing hard.

After a while, I go back. I open it. And there it is:

> i shut the book and stepped away

Then a new line appears, slowly:

> to recap, my name is

And the space sits there, waiting, as if the book were reading back to me what I already have. **[the player writes the name]**

I take the book under my arm, because there is no leaving that thing there, and I go out through the half-open door.
